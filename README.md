# Quant Education

An open-source educational repository by **Acadia Analytics** for developing and sharing quantitative trading knowledge.

## About

This repository contains educational materials covering essential topics in quantitative finance, algorithmic trading, and related mathematical concepts. Our goal is to provide high-quality, accessible content for our users and the broader community of interested learners.

## Content Areas

- **Risk Management** - Capital preservation and portfolio optimization techniques
- **Probability & Statistics** - Mathematical foundations for trading decisions  
- **Machine Learning** - AI applications in algorithmic trading
- **Finance** - Market analysis and investment strategies
