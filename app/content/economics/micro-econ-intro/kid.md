# The Lemonade Stand Price Puzzle 🍋

You set up a lemonade stand on a hot Saturday. You've got a big jug of cold lemonade and a stack of cups. There's only **one** thing left to decide, and it's trickier than it sounds: **what price do you put on the sign?**

Charge too much and everyone walks past. Charge too little and you sell out in ten minutes with barely any money. Somewhere in the middle is the **"just right"** price — the price where the people who want lemonade and the person selling it (you!) actually meet.

## The big idea in one sentence

> **The price isn't too high or too low — it's the spot where the number of cups people want to buy equals the number of cups you want to sell.**

Think about the two ways it can go wrong:

- 💸 **Price too high** → lots of lemonade left, no customers. That's a **surplus** (too much stuff).
- 🕳️ **Price too low** → a line out the door and an empty jug. That's a **shortage** (not enough stuff).

When there's leftover lemonade, you lower the price to get people to buy. When there's a line, you can nudge the price up. Little by little the price slides to the **middle** — and that's exactly how real prices get set, from candy to sneakers to airplane tickets.


```ascii
  $10  |  price too HIGH  ->  🚶 nobody buys   (leftover jug)

       +------------------------------------------------->
                    fewer buyers <---> more buyers
```


## Two more sneaky ideas

**The first cup is the best.** On a hot day the first cup of lemonade is *incredible*. The second is nice. By the fourth, you're kind of done. Each extra cup gives you a little **less** happiness than the one before — which is why almost nobody buys ten cups no matter how cheap they are. Grown-ups call this a fancy name, but you just call it "I'm full."

**Every choice costs something else.** By running the stand all Saturday, you gave up a day of playing video games with your friends. That giving-up is the real "cost" of the stand — even if nobody handed you a bill for it. Smart sellers always ask: *is this worth what I'm giving up?*

So the whole lemonade puzzle is really one balancing act: find the price where buyers and sellers shake hands, remember that the first cup beats the fifth, and never forget what you gave up to be there. 🍋🤝
