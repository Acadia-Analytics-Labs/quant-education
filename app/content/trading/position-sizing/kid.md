# How Much Should You Bet? 🎰

Imagine you get **\$100** for your birthday. A game at the fair lets you win money if you're lucky. The big question isn't *"should I play?"* — it's **"how much of my \$100 should I risk each time?"**

That question has a name in the grown-up world: **position sizing**. It's about *how big* your bet is, not *which* bet you make. And it turns out this is the secret that keeps people from going broke.

## The big idea in one sentence

> **Bet small enough that you're still in the game after a bad streak.**

Real traders have a famous rule: **never risk more than 1–2% of your money on a single bet.** If you have \$100, that means risking about **\$1 to \$2** at a time. Sounds tiny! But watch what happens:

- 🚀 If you bet everything and lose once → you have **nothing**, and you can't win it back.
- 🐢 If you bet a little and lose → you still have almost all your money to try again.

## Why "staying alive" wins

Losing money hurts *more* than winning the same amount helps. If you lose half your money, you don't need to gain 50% to get back — you need to gain **100%**! That's why the smart move is to keep every single loss *small*.


```ascii
  Lose 50% of $100  -->  you have $50
  To get back to $100 you must DOUBLE it (+100%). Ouch.
```


So the whole trick to "how much should I bet?" is really the trick to **not getting knocked out of the game**. Bet small, survive the bad days, and let the good days slowly stack up. 🌱🏆
