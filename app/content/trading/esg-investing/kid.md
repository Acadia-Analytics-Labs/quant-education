# The Tale of Two Lemonade Stands 🍋

Imagine two lemonade stands on the same street. Both sell cold, tasty lemonade for the same price. If you only asked **"how much money did they make today?"** they might look exactly the same.

But that question misses a lot. *How* did they make the money?

## What is ESG?

Grown-ups have a name for looking *beyond* the money at how a company really behaves. They call it **ESG**. It's just three big questions:

- **E is for Environment** 🌍 — How do they treat the **planet**? Do they pollute the river, or recycle and clean up?
- **S is for Social** 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 — How do they treat **people**? Are they fair to workers, safe for customers, and kind to the neighborhood?
- **G is for Governance** ⚖️ — Do they **play fair**? Honest books, no cheating, no lying about what they do.

Stand A scores badly on all three. Stand B scores great.

## Being good can also be smart business

Here's the surprising part: being the *responsible* stand isn't only nice — it can help you **make more money too**.

- Stand A's stinky river gets it a **fine** from the town. That's money gone. 💸
- Stand A's angry helpers quit, so it keeps running out of lemonade.
- Stand B has a great **reputation**, so more customers line up. 😊
- Stand B's happy helpers stick around, so the stand runs smoothly.

So ESG isn't only about being kind. Companies that take care of the planet, their people, and play fair often avoid big trouble down the road.

## But watch out for fibbers! 🕵️

Some stands *say* they recycle but secretly still dump trash. Pretending to be green when you're not has a funny name: **greenwashing**. That's why smart people don't just believe the sign — they check what a company actually does. Looking closely is the whole point of ESG.
