# What Happened in the Markets on Friday, June 5th

On Friday, June 5th, 2026, the US stock market experienced its sharpest sell-off since October. This sell-off ended a nine-week winning streak as the US jobs report fueled concerns of tighter Federal Reserve monetary policy.

The U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics reported that US employers added 172,000 jobs in May, which is around double the expectations of analysts. The unemployment rate remained steady at 4.3%, and the 3-month average job gain was 188,000 jobs.

## Reasons Why the Markets Suffered

The positive jobs report triggered a "good news is bad news" reaction from the markets. When the labor market is strong, the Federal Reserve has little incentive to cut interest rates, since a strong economy does not need stimulus. The markets, though, had been betting on rate cuts later this year.

The bond market reacted immediately. 10-year yields on Treasury notes increased to 4.54%, reflecting that investors think rates will be higher for longer. When yields rise sharply, equities get re-priced lower, especially for high-multiple growth stocks.

There then proceeded to be big tech and AI sell-offs, including Nvidia, AMD, Micron, and Broadcom. Adding to this sell-off, shares of Meta fell 5.5% after a report that they may be considering a new stock offering to fund its AI infrastructure buildout.

## End-of-Day Outcomes

All of these events combined led to:

- The **S&P 500** falling about 200 points
- The **Dow** falling 695 points
- The **Nasdaq** falling over 1,100 points
